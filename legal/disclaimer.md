## tranche_

tranche_ (at reachpointcapital.com/tranche/) shows the capital structure of public companies: their debts, equity and other claims, and how those line up. Please read this section before you rely on anything it shows.

- **Information only.** What tranche_ shows is information. It is not investment advice, an offer, or a recommendation about any company or security, and it does not take your circumstances into account.
- **Extracted by an AI model.** The capital structures are extracted from companies' filings with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) by an AI model, not typed in by hand. A model can miss an instrument, misread a figure, put an amount in the wrong place or get a detail wrong. A result can be incomplete or wrong.
- **The balance-sheet check.** Each result comes with a balance-sheet check. It shows how well the extracted figures tie back to the company's reported balance sheet. A good tie is reassuring but does not prove that every figure is right, and a poor tie is a sign to be careful.
- **Check the filing.** Each figure comes with a quote from the filing it was taken from. Before you rely on a figure, read the quote and the original filing, and confirm it yourself. The filing is the authoritative source, not tranche_.
- **Timing.** A result reflects the filing it was built from. It may be out of date, and it may not reflect later filings, amendments, refinancings, repayments or other events.
- **SEC filings are public documents.** The underlying filings are public and free to read on the SEC's website. tranche_ does not give you any private or inside information.
