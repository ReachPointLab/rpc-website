## Sections keep long notes readable

A research note can run several paragraphs. Section headings give readers somewhere to land, and the reading time at the top of the page tells them what they are getting into.

### Sub-sections work too

Use a third-level heading when a section needs its own parts.

## A numbered list

1. State the question.
2. Show the evidence.
3. Say what would change the view.

> A closing line set apart as a quote.
