Investment Thesis

Built for
the long arc.

Reach Point Capital operates on a permanent capital model. No fund cycles. No forced exits. Decisions made for decades, not quarters.

01
Technology as a capital asset

Software and data products compound in ways that traditional assets don't. RPC treats proprietary technology — both consumer and research — as the foundation of long-term capital formation.

02
Research-driven decision making

Meridian and Reach Point Research exist to institutionalize the research process — building the infrastructure to generate and act on original macro and equity insights at scale.

03
Audience before assets under management

Distribution is a moat. Building authentic relationships with health-conscious consumers and research-driven investors creates strategic leverage well before traditional fund structures are warranted.
